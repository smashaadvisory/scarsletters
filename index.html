    Meet the Founders

    We are Toni and Marguerite, two friends with a great appetite for literature, and a particular fondness for the sort of writing that gets its author into trouble. We both read and write a great deal, and have come to enjoy the peculiar intimacy that develops when we make ourselves available to another person’s imagination. There is a wonderful sort of play involved in this, especially when it comes to writing about oneself. We may begin with every intention of giving an honest account of some experience, only to discover that we have lied, or misunderstood ourselves, or inadvertently revealed something rather incriminating. How embarrassing! But we like this, and find that even the most earnest attempts at self examination can produce all sorts of wonderful absurdities.

    The Scarlet Letter grew out of our mutual enjoyment of this practice, and our desire to act as a sort of hydraulic pressure release valve for people that made something interesting of their private lives, but feel too afraid to share it unabashedly. We are interested in what happens when the more obscure parts of our experience are brought into language and literature, what sorts of strange sympathies might develop between people, who would otherwise never know one another. As for subject matter, we are quite happy to entertain the indecorous, the brutally honest, the perverse, the blasphemous, the taboo, really whatever else has been deemed unfit for polite conversation. We have no particular interest in passing harsh moral judgement on our contributors, which would be an absurd undertaking considering how little we can really know about anyone, ourselves included. Besides, a good account of a bad thing can be excellent literature, as history has proven!

    We would like to read whatever you have been writing, including the things you have kept to yourself out of modesty, embarrassment, fear, or any other interesting motives. We are very fond of the written word, and we are terribly curious about people. It seemed natural to bring these two affections together.
