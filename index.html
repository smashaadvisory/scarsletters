About The Scarlet Letter

We are both a little too fond of the sort of writing that begins as an account of some perfectly ordinary occurrence and ends with the author having implicated herself rather badly. This seems inevitable. To describe a feeling honestly is to find that it has developed an appetite of its own, and that one’s memories are not especially obedient witnesses. In conversation, there is always an opportunity to change the subject. On paper, one is less fortunate. We like this about it.

Toni and Marguerite share this affection for personal writing, particularly the sort in which the author finds herself revealing rather more than she intended. We thought it might be nice to collect such things, and to read what other people have made of themselves in the privacy of their own pages. Hence this little enterprise.

— Love, Toni & Marguerite
